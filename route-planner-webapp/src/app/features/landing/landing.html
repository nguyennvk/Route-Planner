It decides in a fixed order of priority

A shorter route never wins by overloading a van or missing a promise. Each level only counts once the one above it is settled.

  1. First

    Keep the hard rules

    No vehicle goes over its weight limit. Orders marked as strict arrive inside their delivery window. If a plan ever can't meet these, the screen tells you plainly.

  2. Then

    Deliver as many orders as possible

    Leaving an order behind costs more than any amount of extra driving.

  3. Finally

    Cut the cost of the day

    Less total driving, measured with real road travel times. On a flexible window, a minute late counts the same as five minutes behind the wheel.

The search stops after 5 seconds with no improvement, and after 30 seconds at most.
